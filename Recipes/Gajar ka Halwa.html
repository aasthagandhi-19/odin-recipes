Gajar Ka Halwa

Description

This Punjabi dessert is subtle and divine without being super sweet. Making gajar ka halwa is a labor of love — don’t rush. Enjoy the process. It will be worth it!

Ingredients

  • 3 cups whole milk
  • 4 to 6 tablespoons ghee
  • 1/3 cup nuts (pistachios, almonds or cashews)
  • 1½ pounds carrots, grated
  • ½ teaspoon crushed cardamom seeds (optional)
  • ¾ cup light brown sugar

Directions

  1. Bring the milk to boil and reduce until thickened.
  2. Fry nuts in ghee, set aside.
  3. Sauté carrots in the same skillet until softened.
  4. Add reduced milk and cardamom, simmer till absorbed.
  5. Add sugar and cook until thickened.
  6. Finish with ghee and garnish with nuts.
  7. Reheat and caramelize before serving, if desired.
