Pani Puri

Description

Pani puri is a popular street food in India. It consists of fragile puffed wafers that are popped and stuffed with a spiced mixture of chickpeas and potato, then topped off with flavored water, which is the most important part.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup peeled, cubed green mango
  • 2 Thai green or serrano chilies, roughly chopped
  • 1 bunch cilantro, leaves and tender stems roughly chopped
  • 1/2 bunch mint, leaves picked
  • Juice from 3 limes
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons roasted ground cumin
  • 1 tablespoon fine Indian black salt (kala namak)
  • Fine sea salt
  • Sugar or jaggery, to taste
  • 2 tablespoons dried black chickpeas (kala chana), soaked overnight
  • A pinch of baking soda
  • 2 medium Yukon gold potatoes
  • 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 1/4 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon fine Indian black salt (kala namak)
  • Juice from half a lime
  • 30 Pani Puri shells

Directions

  1. For the green mango water: Combine the green mango, chilies, cilantro, mint, lime juice, and 1/2 cup water in a food processor. Blend until smooth. Pour into a bowl and add the cumin, black salt, 1 1/2 teaspoons sea salt, and 3 cups water. Whisk to combine. Add sugar to taste.
  2. For the potato filling: Boil the soaked black chickpeas and potatoes until tender. Peel and mash the potatoes. Add chickpeas, cilantro, cumin, black salt, chili powder, lime juice, and 1/2 teaspoon sea salt. Mix well.
  3. For assembly: Preheat the oven to 225°F. Bake the Pani Puri shells until crispy, 3-5 minutes.
  4. To serve: Crack a hole in each shell, fill with the potato mixture, and top with green mango water. Eat the assembled Pani Puris immediately before they become soggy.
