Stuffed Eggplant

Description

This delicious stuffed eggplant with a cheesy top is a must-try recipe! Great as a hearty main but you can buy mini eggplants and use them for a side dish or appetizer.

Ingredients

  • 1 (1½ pound) eggplant, halved lengthwise
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1/2 pound sweet Italian sausage, casings removed
  • 1 cup chopped onion
  • 1 cup sliced fresh mushrooms
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh basil
  • 1 cup tomato-basil pasta sauce
  • 3/4 cup Italian seasoned panko bread crumbs, divided
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese, divided

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a rimmed baking sheet with foil.
  2. Cut around the edge of the flesh in each eggplant half, leaving a 1/2-inch border. Scoop out flesh, leaving 1/2-inch-thick shell; chop flesh into bite-size pieces.
  3. Brush eggplant shells with 2 tablespoons olive oil, season with salt and pepper, and set aside.
  4. Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in a skillet. Add sausage, onion, mushrooms, and garlic; cook until sausage is browned. Stir in chopped eggplant and basil. Add pasta sauce and cook for 5 minutes. Mix in 1/2 cup of panko and 1/4 cup Parmesan cheese.
  5. Stuff mixture into eggplant shells. Combine remaining panko and Parmesan in a bowl, sprinkle over stuffing.
  6. Bake for 45 to 50 minutes until eggplant is tender.
